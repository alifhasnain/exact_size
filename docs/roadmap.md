# Roadmap & Prioritized Backlog

> Backlog filled in Stage 3 (`/discover-features`), scored in Stage 4 (`/prioritize`).
> Rejected features stay in the table with status `Rejected`; IDs are never reused.

## Scoring
- **RICE** = Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort
  - Reach: users affected per quarter
  - Impact: 3 massive · 2 high · 1 medium · 0.5 low · 0.25 minimal
  - Confidence: 100% / 80% / 50%
  - Effort: person-weeks
- **MoSCoW** (MVP only): Must · Should · Could · Won't (this release)
- **Release**: MVP · Next · Later

**Scoring conventions (Stage 4, all Proposed)**
- Reach: no usage data exists, so we assume 10,000 quarterly active users at launch [Assumption]. Reach is the estimated users per quarter who touch the feature (10,000 = every user). Sub-populations (non-English users, operators, face photos) are also [Assumption].
- Confidence 50% = hypothesis only (for example operator demand A3, ad revenue, conversion); 80% = research-backed but not validated; 100% = certain need.
- Effort = person-weeks for one Flutter developer working with Claude Code, including tests. Store release work (listings in 6 languages, privacy labels, device QA, review cycles) is not a backlog row and is budgeted separately at 1.5 pw in M1.
- RICE is rounded to whole numbers. Table is sorted by RICE descending; Rejected rows are at the bottom.
- The MVP cut is driven by the 8-10 pw solo budget, not by RICE alone. Prerequisites the product cannot ship without are Must regardless of score.
