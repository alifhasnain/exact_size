## UX strengths
Per-form landing pages found via search; generous free tier. `[Hypothesis]`

## UX weaknesses / user complaints
No review evidence gathered. Page lists PDF/HEIC in upload but not in FAQ (inconsistent). No signature tool mentioned on this page. "Best compression method" for KB unspecified.

## What they don't do (gaps)
Native/offline use, min KB range stated, saved custom presets, signature cleanup (on this page).

## Takeaways for us
`[Hypothesis]` This is the default behaviour to beat: free, no account, many form pages. Our edge must be speed, offline, and trust.
