# PhotoAiD

## Snapshot
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | direct for the visa/passport segment |
| Platforms | iOS, Android, web [Fact](https://photoaid.com/visas-and-other-documents-needed-abroad) |
| Markets | Global; the visa page lists about 12 countries/regions plus document types (partial page view) [Fact](https://photoaid.com/visas-and-other-documents-needed-abroad) |
| Business model | Free to download; pay per photo; sources disagree on subscriptions/IAP and one review summary flags unclear pricing [Fact](https://www.g2.com/products/photoaid/reviews) |
| Scale signals | Page shows 4.7 from 82.6K reviews (store unspecified); Trustpilot about 4.7; App Store about 4.5 per an aggregator [Fact](https://photoaid.com/visas-and-other-documents-needed-abroad) |
| Sources | Vendor page (primary), G2/Trustpilot (reviews, secondary) |

## Core features
- AI validator checks the photo against the selected document; auto crop, framing, background `[Fact](https://photoaid.com/visas-and-other-documents-needed-abroad)`
- Flags closed eyes, open mouth; free retake on failure `[Fact](https://photoaid.com/visas-and-other-documents-needed-abroad)`

## UX strengths
Compliance focus (content rules) and a guided flow; high ratings.

## UX weaknesses / user complaints
Reviews mention photos rejected by an issuing office after paying, and pricing that ended up higher than quoted; some doubt savings over a pharmacy; privacy worries named in G2 summary `[Fact](https://www.g2.com/products/photoaid/reviews)`.

## What they don't do (gaps)
Upload storage and retention not stated on the page. Not aimed at exact-KB exam or job forms; page shows no KB targeting.

## Takeaways for us
`[Hypothesis]` Content/biometric compliance is a different, costly (AI, per-photo) problem. For MVP, size-and-KB presets for visa portals may be enough; do not promise acceptance.
