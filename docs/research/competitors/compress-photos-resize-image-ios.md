# Compress Photos - Resize Image (iOS)

## Snapshot
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | direct (generic KB compressor) |

## Core features
- Compress to KB or any chosen size; adjustable JPEG strength; before/after preview `[Fact](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6444087511)`
- Batch: free tier up to 5 photos at a time; Pro removes limit and ads `[Fact](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6444087511)`
- Says photos are processed on device; privacy label "Data Not Collected" (developer-declared, unverified, and odd for an ad-supported app) `[Fact](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6444087511)`

## UX strengths
Simple flow; preview of saved space. `[Hypothesis]`

## UX weaknesses / user complaints
Only one visible review: too many ads and a limited number of selectable photos `[Fact](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6444087511)`.
