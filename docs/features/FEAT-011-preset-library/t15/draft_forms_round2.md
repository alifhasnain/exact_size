# DRAFT round 2 — unverified research, not for the app until the Product Owner confirms each row against the source

- Read on 2026-11-27 for every row. Values are only what the cited page states; nothing is converted or guessed. Round 1 (rows 1–15) is in `draft_forms.md`; none of those forms is repeated.
- Scope this round: forms whose official notice states an exact pixel size. Forms giving only KB, cm/mm, a minimum or a pixel range are listed at the end.
- Confidence: High = official issuer page or PDF, explicit, current cycle. Medium = official but older cycle, soft wording, or numbers partly unreadable. Third-party-only rows were dropped.
- No personal data is involved: these are public form rules.

Summary: 7 forms (target was 8–12; see "Access limits" at the end). IN 4, BD 2, SG 1. High 3, Medium 4.

## 16. in-sbi-recruitment
- **Name / issuer:** State Bank of India recruitment applications (Specialist Cadre Officers; same block in the PO advertisement). Issuer: State Bank of India, Central Recruitment & Promotion Department.
- **Country / category:** IN / job
- **Source:** "Engagement of Specialist Cadre Officers on Contract Basis (Advertisement No: CRPD/SCO/2026-27/13)", online registration 07.08.2026–27.08.2026, https://sbi.bank.in/documents/77530/57941334/FINAL++ADVT++CRPD_SCO_2026-27_13.pdf/64d9efd8-2a64-ad88-d4a1-0ae1a5e2fb4b. Cross-check: "Recruitment of Probationary Officers (Advertisement No: CRPD/PO/2024-25/22)", 27.12.2024–16.01.2025, https://sbi.co.in/documents/77530/43947057/26122024_Detailed_Adv.2024_27.12.2024.pdf. Read 2026-11-27.
- **Photo:** 200 × 230 px ("preferred"); 20–50 KB; JPEG (the upload procedure says to save as .jpeg).
- **Signature:** 140 × 60 px ("preferred"); 10–20 KB; JPEG.
- **Extra rules:** recent passport-style colour photo; light-coloured, preferably white background; no caps, hats or dark glasses; religious headwear allowed if the face is not covered; glasses without reflections. Signature in black ink on white paper, signed only by the applicant. The PO advertisement adds: signature in CAPITAL LETTERS not accepted; left thumb impression 240 × 240 px (20–50 KB) and hand-written declaration 800 × 400 px (50–100 KB), out of scope.
- **Quote:** "Size of file should be between 20 kb - 50 kb and Dimensions 200 x 230 pixels (preferred)" / "Size of file should be between 10 kb - 20 kb and Dimensions 140 x 60 pixels (preferred)."
- **Notes:** Same numbers as row 3 (IBPS). The PO could merge them into one preset with both names. "preferred" means the pixel size may not be enforced exactly. SBI Clerk 2026 advertisement not read (third-party pages claim the same values).
- **Confidence:** High

## 17. in-embassy-consular-photo
- **Name / issuer:** Photo and signature upload for the Embassy of India (Paris) applicant portal (consular/passport services; the portal is not named in the document). Issuer: Embassy of India, Paris (Ministry of External Affairs).
- **Country / category:** IN / visa_passport
- **Source:** "Practical guidelines for uploading photo and signature", https://www.eoiparis.gov.in/content/Practical_Guidelines_Uploading_Photo_Signature_29_April_26.pdf (file name dated 29 April 2026). Read 2026-11-27.
- **Photo:** 630 × 810 px; max "upto 200 kb" (soft wording); min not stated; JPG.
- **Signature:** px not stated; max "upto 100 kb"; JPG.
- **Extra rules:** solid white background; face 80–85% of the frame (top of hair to chin); natural expression, taken from about 1.5 m. Signature in black or dark blue ink on plain white paper, cropped so it covers 80–85% of the box, grey background removed.
- **Quote:** "Step 2: Re-size to ICAO Dimensions (630 x 810 pixels)" / "Generally, you should be able to upload upto 200 kb for photo and upto 100 kb for signature."
- **Notes:** "Generally" makes the KB limits soft. Signature has KB only, so it needs a chosen exact px value. **Privacy flag:** the guide tells applicants to use online tools (remove.bg, iloveimg.com, zoho, adobe) for background removal and compression, which sends a passport photo to third parties; the app's local processing avoids this. Whether other Indian missions use the same values was not checked (Embassy of India Brussels SOP only gives a 1 MB total for documents).
- **Confidence:** Medium
